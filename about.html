Our Story

Over 28 Years of Educational Excellence

Founded in 1998, Greenfield Comprehensive School began as a small Nursery and Primary school along the Lekki-Epe Expressway with just 30 pupils. Today, we are a full-fledged Creche-to-SSS3 institution, proudly serving over 1,450 students drawn from across Nigeria and beyond.

We remain rooted in the belief that every child deserves a nurturing, safe, and academically rigorous environment built on strong Nigerian values, discipline, and global competitiveness.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, values-based education that equips every child with the knowledge, skills, and character to excel academically and lead responsibly in society.

Our Vision

To be the leading indigenous school of choice in Nigeria, recognised for producing disciplined, innovative, and globally competitive graduates.

Our Core Values

Discipline, Excellence, Integrity, Respect, and God-Consciousness — the pillars upon which every Greenfield pupil is raised.

Message From Leadership

Our Commitment to Your Child's Future

At Greenfield, we understand that parents entrust us with their most valuable possession — their children. This is a responsibility we do not take lightly. Our seasoned educators, modern facilities, and structured curriculum work hand-in-hand to prepare pupils for Common Entrance, BECE, WAEC, and NECO examinations, while also grooming them into well-rounded, respectful young Nigerians.

We continually invest in staff training, ICT infrastructure, and extracurricular programmes to ensure that every child who passes through Greenfield leaves better prepared for the future — academically, socially, and morally.

Mrs. Adaeze Okonkwo-Bello

Proprietress & Principal
