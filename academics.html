Nigerian National Curriculum

Our Curriculum Structure

Greenfield delivers the full Nigerian National Curriculum as approved by the Federal Ministry of Education, across two divisions: Primary and Secondary.

Primary School (Creche – Basic 6)

Play-based early learning focusing on Letter & Number Recognition, Rhymes, Phonics, Basic Hygiene, Social Habits, and Creative Play. Emphasis on sensory development and school readiness.

English Studies, Mathematics, Basic Science & Technology, National Values Education (Civic & Social Studies), Nigerian Language (Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa), Cultural & Creative Arts, Christian/Islamic Religious Studies, Handwriting and Phonics.

English Studies, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, National Values Education, Nigerian Language, Cultural & Creative Arts, Physical & Health Education, Computer Studies, French, and Common Entrance Examination preparatory classes.

Secondary School (JSS 1 – SSS 3)

English Language, Mathematics, Basic Science, Basic Technology, Civic Education, Social Studies, Business Studies, Agricultural Science, Home Economics, French, Nigerian Language, Christian/Islamic Religious Studies, Data Processing (Computer Studies), Physical & Health Education, Cultural & Creative Arts. Culminates in the Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE).

English Language, General Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Further Mathematics, Agricultural Science, Data Processing, Civic Education, Geography. Prepares students for WAEC, NECO and JAMB/UTME science-related courses.

Arts: Literature-in-English, Government, History, CRS/IRS, Fine Art, French.
Commercial: Financial Accounting, Commerce, Economics, Marketing, Office Practice.
All tracks include English Language, General Mathematics, Civic Education, and Data Processing. Prepares students for WAEC and NECO examinations.
