A Word From Our Proprietress

Welcome to Greenfield Comprehensive School

Dear Parents and Guardians,

It is with great pleasure that I welcome you to Greenfield Comprehensive School, a home away from home for your children. Since our establishment, we have remained committed to raising disciplined, God-fearing, and academically sound individuals who are well prepared to face the challenges of the 21st century, both within Nigeria and on the global stage.

Our qualified and dedicated teaching staff work tirelessly to ensure every child — from Creche to SSS 3 — receives quality, values-driven education anchored on the Nigerian National Curriculum. We invite you to partner with us in raising the leaders of tomorrow.

Mrs. Adaeze Okonkwo-Bello

Proprietress & Principal, Greenfield Comprehensive School

Our Academic Divisions

Two Sections, One Standard of Excellence

Greenfield runs a full continuum of Nigerian basic and post-basic education, carefully structured to match each child's developmental stage.

Primary School

Covering Creche, Nursery 1–2, and Primary/Basic 1–6, our early years and primary programme lays a strong foundation in literacy, numeracy, and moral values.

  • Creche & Nursery (Play-based Early Learning)
  • Basic 1 – 6 (Nigerian National Curriculum)
  • Phonics, Handwriting & Reading Clinics
  • Common Entrance Examination Preparation
  • Sporting, Music & Creative Arts Clubs
View Primary Curriculum

Secondary School

Our Junior and Senior Secondary sections (JSS 1–3 and SSS 1–3) prepare students rigorously for BECE, WAEC, and NECO, alongside university and career guidance.

  • JSS 1 – 3 (Junior Secondary / BECE Prep)
  • SSS 1 – 3 (Science, Arts & Commercial Tracks)
  • WAEC & NECO Examination Preparation
  • Career Counselling & JAMB/UTME Guidance
  • Debate, JETS Club & Sports Teams
View Secondary Curriculum
